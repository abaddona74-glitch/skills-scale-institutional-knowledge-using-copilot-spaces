# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers design user experiences and interfaces, ensuring usability and intuitive workflows for all product features.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research to understand customer needs and pain points
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and user flows
- Perform usability testing and gather user feedback
- Collaborate on design reviews with Product Managers and Product Managers
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design standards
- Document design patterns and maintain design systems

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-friendly experiences
- Validate designs with real users before implementation
- Maintain consistency across product features

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with PM, PdM, and Developers
- User research findings and usability test reports
- Wireframes and prototypes shared via design tools
- Design specifications and handoff documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate to validate user needs and ensure designs align with product vision
- **Developers**: Work together on implementation feasibility and design handoff
- **QA/Testing**: Partner on usability validation and acceptance testing
- **Data Analyst**: Use analytics insights to inform design decisions

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts turn raw data into actionable insights to guide product decisions and process improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Analyze usage metrics, user behavior, and success criteria
- Support PM/PdM with reporting and data-driven insights
- Identify data-driven risks and opportunities
- Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Instrument analytics and work with developers on data collection
- Create dashboards and visualizations for stakeholders

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision making across teams
- Measure and communicate product/project success
- Identify improvement opportunities through data patterns

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reports and dashboard updates
- Data analysis presentations for planning and retrospectives
- Ad-hoc analysis requests from PM/PdM
- Recommendations based on usage patterns

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Define success metrics and provide insights for prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Support project tracking with velocity and delivery metrics
- **Developers**: Collaborate on analytics instrumentation and data quality
- **UX Designer**: Share user behavior data to inform design decisions

---

## QA Automation Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Automation Engineers build, maintain, and improve automated test frameworks to ensure consistent quality assurance.

### Responsibilities
- Develop end-to-end, regression, and performance test suites
- Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines
- Partner with Developers on test coverage and quality standards
- Maintain test frameworks and infrastructure
- Identify and report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Support manual QA team with automation priorities

### Goals
- Maximize test coverage and reduce manual testing burden
- Enable fast feedback cycles for developers
- Prevent regressions and catch defects early

### Typical Communication
- Test reports and coverage metrics in CI/CD
- Collaboration with developers on testability
- Test plan reviews during sprint planning
- Defect reports and quality dashboards

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on writing testable code and implementing test automation
- **QA/Testing**: Coordinate on automation priorities and complement manual testing
- **Project Managers**: Provide quality metrics for release decisions
- **Product Managers**: Ensure automated tests cover critical user flows

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes, remove delivery impediments, and promote team health and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Track and resolve impediments blocking team progress
- Coach team on agile principles and best practices
- Ensure process adherence and suggest improvements
- Foster collaboration and psychological safety
- Protect team from distractions and unplanned work

### Goals
- Maintain consistent delivery cadence
- Maximize team productivity and morale
- Continuously improve team processes

### Typical Communication
- Daily facilitation of standups and team meetings
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Sprint reports and velocity metrics
- Coaching conversations with team members

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on delivery schedules and cross-team dependencies
- **Product Managers**: Ensure backlog is ready and priorities are clear
- **Developers**: Help remove blockers and improve development workflow
- **QA/Testing**: Support quality processes and testing workflow optimization
- **All Team Members**: Foster collaboration and address team dynamics
